Questions

Frequently asked

Everything below reflects what the app actually enforces today — no roadmap promises.

BillingCan I cancel anytime?

Yes. While signed in, open Manage Subscription on this page. It takes you to Stripe’s customer portal, where you can cancel, change your card, or review past invoices. You’re never charged again, and Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Free tierIs there a free trial?

There’s no trial, because the free tier is genuinely free forever — no card, no expiry, no countdown. Signing in with Google raises your allowance from 5 to 10 enhancements a week and unlocks Memories and the 300-template prompt library.

Free tierDo I need an account to use Prompt Helper?

No. Signed out you get 5 enhancements a week and can see your most recent result. Signing in with Google raises that to 10 a week, unlocks Memories and the 300-template prompt library, and gives you 10 items of history instead of 1. It costs nothing.

Chrome extensionDoes Pro work on the website and the extension?

Yes — one Pro subscription covers both. You do not need a separate extension plan, and the extension is not a separate purchase.

Your account is keyed to your Google identity, so the moment you sign in to the extension with the same Google account, it picks up your Pro status automatically — unlimited enhancements, full history, and custom styles inside the extension. There is nothing extra to buy and nothing extra to link.

One thing worth knowing: your generated prompts stay in the browser you created them in. We don’t upload them, so your saved prompts and history do not travel between devices — but your plan, your allowance, and your Pro status do.

BillingWhat payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards, processed by Stripe. Checkout is secure and card details never touch our servers.

PlansDo you offer team plans?

Not yet — Pro is a single-user plan right now. If you need seats for a team, email support@promptgenerator.fyi and we’ll tell you where we are with that.

PlansIs there a lifetime deal?

Not currently. Pro is $19.99/month and you can cancel any time. If we ever add a one-time lifetime option, we’ll announce it here first.

UsageWhat exactly counts as one enhancement?

One prompt you send through a generator. If you turn on A/B variants, that single run returns two versions of your prompt — it still counts as one enhancement, not two.

FeaturesWhat are Memories, and what do A/B variants do?

Memories store personal preferences — your role, tone, industry — and apply them to every prompt you generate, so you stop repeating yourself.

A/B variants return two versions of your prompt side by side: your chosen style plus an alternative, so you can compare and pick. This one is Pro-only — on Free the toggle is visible but locked, so you can see exactly what upgrading adds.

PrivacyWhere do my prompts and history live?

In your browser, on the device you used. Your generated prompts and history are stored locally and are never uploaded to us. Clearing your browser data clears them. The only thing tied to your account is your plan, allowance, and Memories.

BillingHow do I request a refund?

Email support@promptgenerator.fyi with the email on the account and the date of charge. We process refund requests within a few business days.
