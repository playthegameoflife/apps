Questions

Frequently asked

Everything below reflects what the app actually enforces today — no roadmap promises.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. While signed in, open Manage Subscription on this page. It takes you to Stripe’s customer portal, where you can cancel, change your card, or review past invoices. You’re never charged again, and Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Is there a free trial?

There’s no trial, because the free tier is genuinely free forever — no card, no expiry, no countdown. Signing in with Google raises your allowance from 5 to 10 enhancements a week and unlocks Memories and A/B variants.

Do I need an account to use Prompt Helper?

No. Signed out you get 5 enhancements a week and can see your most recent result. Signing in with Google raises that to 10 a week, unlocks Memories and A/B variants, and gives you 10 items of history instead of 1. It costs nothing.

Does Pro work on the website and the extension?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account on both and your Pro subscription follows you automatically.

One thing worth knowing: your generated prompts stay in the browser you created them in. We don’t upload them, so history doesn’t travel between devices — but your plan and allowance do.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards, processed by Stripe. Checkout is secure and card details never touch our servers.

Do you offer team plans?

Not yet — Pro is a single-user plan right now. If you need seats for a team, email support@promptgenerator.fyi and we’ll tell you where we are with that.

Is there a lifetime deal?

Not currently. Pro is $19.99/month and you can cancel any time. If we ever add a one-time lifetime option, we’ll announce it here first.

What exactly counts as one enhancement?

One prompt you send through a generator. If you turn on A/B variants, that single run returns two versions of your prompt — it still counts as one enhancement, not two.

What are Memories and A/B variants?

Memories store personal preferences — your role, tone, industry — and apply them to every prompt you generate, so you stop repeating yourself.

A/B variants return two versions of your prompt side by side: your chosen style plus an alternative, so you can compare and pick. Both are included on Free and Pro.

Where do my prompts and history live?

In your browser, on the device you used. Your generated prompts and history are stored locally and are never uploaded to us. Clearing your browser data clears them. The only thing tied to your account is your plan, allowance, and Memories.

How do I request a refund?

Email support@promptgenerator.fyi with the email on the account and the date of charge. We process refund requests within a few business days.
