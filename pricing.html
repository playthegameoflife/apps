Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings and you won't be charged again. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.

Is there a free trial?

The free tier is free forever — no trial needed. Guests get 5 enhancements per week with no credit card and no account. Sign in with Google (also free) to double that to 10 a week and unlock Memory and A/B variants.

A free Google account doubles your weekly allowance from 5 to 10 enhancements, raises your history from 1 item to 10, and unlocks Memories (personalization) and A/B variants. It costs nothing and your allowance is tracked on your account, so it follows you across devices.

Does Pro work on both the website and the extension?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account on both and your Pro subscription syncs automatically.
