How Postiz AI Automation Works

1. AI Caption Generation

Upload an image or video, and Postiz AI generates 3–5 caption variants optimized for different platforms. It respects character limits (X's 280 chars, LinkedIn's 3,000, Instagram's 2,200), tone of voice settings you configure, and even platform-specific hashtag styles (Instagram hashtag blocks vs. X's inline mentions).

You can regenerate, edit in-line, or approve all variants in one click. For a social media manager who previously spent 10 minutes per caption, this alone saves 30–60 minutes per content batch.

2. Queue-Based Scheduling

Instead of scheduling individual posts, you build a queue: a pool of content that auto-populates a calendar based on your posting frequency rules. Set "one post per day, 9 AM EST, Monday through Friday" and Postiz pulls from your queue automatically.

Queues mean you can batch-create a full month's content in a single session and trust the system to publish it on schedule. No more "I forgot to post on Thursday." No more scrambling on a Sunday night.

3. Team Collaboration & Approval Workflows

For agencies and brands with multiple team members, Postiz supports role-based access: admins create and approve, editors draft, viewers read-only. You can require approval before any post goes live — critical for brands with compliance requirements or multiple stakeholders reviewing copy.

4. Recurring Post Automation

Create a post once and mark it as "recurring" — daily, weekly, or on custom intervals. Evergreen content (motivational quotes, product tips, industry statistics) gets scheduled once and perpetually republished without any manual re-entry.

How to Set Up Postiz AI Automation in 15 Minutes

Getting started with Postiz takes about 15 minutes if you already have a server. Here's the fast-track setup:

Step 1: Deploy via Docker

Terminal — one-command deploy docker run -d \ --name postiz \ -p 3000:3000 \ -e DATABASE_URL=file:./db.sqlite \ postiz/postiz:latest

After the container starts, open http://your-server-ip:3000 and create your admin account.

Step 2: Connect Your First Platform

In the Postiz dashboard, go to Channels → Add Channel. Select your platform (e.g., Instagram, LinkedIn, X). Postiz uses official OAuth flows, so you'll authorize directly with the platform — no third-party credential sharing. Repeat for each channel you want to manage.

Step 3: Configure AI Caption Settings

Go to Settings → AI. Set your default tone of voice (professional, casual, witty), your brand hashtags, and any platform-specific rules. These preferences get applied every time AI generates captions for you.

Step 4: Build Your First Queue

Go to Queues → Create Queue. Set your posting schedule:

  • Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM EST
  • Saturday, 10:00 AM EST
  • Frequency: 1 post per day

Now create your first batch of content: upload images, let AI generate captions, review and edit, then click Add to Queue. Postiz handles the rest.

Step 5: Invite Your Team

Go to Team → Invite Member. Assign roles: Admin (full access), Editor (can draft and schedule), or Viewer (read-only). For client work, create a separate Workspace per client to keep calendars and content isolated.
