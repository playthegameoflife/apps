How Postiz AI Agent Scheduling Works

Postiz runs on a queue-based publishing model. The AI agent adds posts to a queue, and you review them in the visual calendar before they go live. This review step is the key difference between fully automated and semi-automated scheduling.

The Three-Step Agent Workflow

  1. Goal Setting — You define content themes, posting frequency, and target audiences for each platform. The agent uses these parameters to generate relevant content ideas.
  2. Draft Generation — The AI creates posts with platform-specific captions, hashtag suggestions, and optimal scheduling times. It can also generate AI images and short video clips relevant to your content.
  3. Review and Publish — Each draft lands in your calendar queue. You approve, edit, or reject with a single click. Approved posts publish automatically at their scheduled time.

Setting Up Your First AI Agent in Postiz

Getting started takes less than 10 minutes if you use the cloud version. Here is the step-by-step.

Step 1: Connect Your Social Accounts

Navigate to the Connections section in Postiz and authorize each platform you want to post to. Postiz supports OAuth connections for all major networks. You do not need to provide passwords — each platform handles its own authentication securely.

Supported platforms include: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Pages and Groups, X, LinkedIn Personal and Company pages, Threads, Reddit, Pinterest, Google Business, Bluesky, Mastodon, Nostr, Discord, Slack, and GitLab.

Step 2: Configure the AI Agent

Go to the Agents section and create a new agent. You will set parameters including content themes (e.g., "fitness tips, product announcements, behind-the-scenes"), posting frequency per platform, and tone of voice (professional, casual, humorous). The agent reads these settings and generates content aligned with your brand.

Example Agent Configuration { "name": "Fitness Content Agent", "themes": ["workout tips", "nutrition facts", "product reviews"], "posts_per_week": { "instagram": 5, "tiktok": 3, "youtube": 1, "twitter": 7 }, "tone": "motivational and educational", "include_hashtags": true, "add_ai_images": true }

Step 3: Review the Draft Queue

Once the agent generates drafts, they appear in your calendar view. The queue shows each pending post with its platform icon, scheduled time, caption preview, and media thumbnail. Click any draft to edit the caption, swap the image, adjust the timing, or add platform-specific notes.

Automating Evergreen Content Recycling

One of the most powerful Postiz features for creators is ReQueue — the ability to automatically resurface evergreen posts. Instead of manually re-scheduling your best-performing content, set it to recycle on a schedule you define.

For example, a workout tutorial that performed well in January can be set to repost every 90 days automatically. Your top-performing posts become a perpetual content engine rather than a one-time effort. This is especially valuable for accounts with smaller content libraries that want to maintain consistent posting frequency without creating new content every single day.
