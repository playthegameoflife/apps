How to Schedule TikTok Posts with Postiz

Step 1: Connect Your TikTok Account

After installing Postiz (more on that below), navigate to Channels and select TikTok from the available integrations. Postiz uses TikTok's official API to connect, which means your account credentials stay secure and the integration is always up to date with TikTok's latest API policies.

Authorize the connection and you are ready to start scheduling. Postiz supports both personal TikTok accounts and Business accounts — Business accounts get access to the native scheduling API which gives you precise time control.

Step 2: Use the Queue System to Auto-Publish at Peak Times

One of Postiz's most powerful features is its queue system. Instead of manually setting a publish time for each individual post, you add posts to a queue and Postiz distributes them across your scheduled time slots automatically.

This is a game-changer for TikTok creators who batch-produce content. Shoot 10 videos on a Sunday afternoon. Upload them all to Postiz. Add them all to the queue. Postiz publishes them at your optimal posting times throughout the week — no manual intervention needed each day.

Postiz Queue Logic # How the queue works: # 1. You define time slots (e.g., 9am, 12pm, 6pm, 9pm) # 2. Posts in the queue fill those slots in order # 3. When one slot fires, the next queued post publishes automatically # 4. Set recycle rules to re-queue top performers Queue: [Post A] → fires at 9am Mon → published Queue: [Post B] → fires at 12pm Mon → published Queue: [Post C] → fires at 6pm Mon → published Queue: [Post D] → fires at 9am Tue → queued

Step 3: Cross-Post One Video to TikTok + Instagram + YouTube

The single biggest time-saver in Postiz is cross-posting. Create one post, attach your video, and select multiple channels — TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts all get their own adapted version of your caption with platform-appropriate hashtags.

You can also set platform-specific notes: Instagram might need a call-to-action that TikTok does not, and Postiz handles that with per-channel caption overrides.
