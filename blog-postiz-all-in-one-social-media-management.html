What Is Postiz? A Complete Social Media Hub

Postiz is an open-source, agentic social media scheduling platform that consolidates what most creators pay for through multiple subscriptions into one dashboard. Launched in September 2024 by indie developer Nevo David, it reached $1.3 million ARR by early 2026 — a rare success story for open-source social media tooling.

Unlike closed-source competitors like Hootsuite ($99/month+) or Metricool ($45/month), Postiz gives you the source code. You can run the cloud version for convenience or deploy it on your own VPS for the cost of a $5-10 server. It supports 30+ platforms including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest, Google Business, Mastodon, Bluesky, Discord, and GitLab.

The platform's "agentic" positioning means its AI doesn't just assist — it actively plans, generates, and schedules posts, with you reviewing everything in a visual calendar before anything goes live.

The Tool Stack Problem: Why Creators Are Consolidating

Postiz Core Features: What's Included

1. Visual Content Calendar

Postiz's calendar view lets you see all scheduled posts across every platform in one place. Drag-and-drop rescheduling, bulk queue management, and timezone-aware publishing ensure your content goes live exactly when your audience is active — whether you're managing one account or fifty.

2. AI-Powered Content Generation

Every plan includes AI agent access. The built-in AI copilot generates post captions, rewrites existing content for different platforms, suggests hashtags, and can even auto-generate content ideas based on your previous posts. Rather than copying a tweet into ChatGPT and back, you write once inside Postiz and the AI adapts it for each network's format and audience.

3. Queue and Evergreen Content Recycling

Set up content queues that automatically pull from your library on a schedule. The evergreen content feature lets you mark posts as "always relevant" and have them automatically re-queued after a defined interval — turning your best content into a perpetual traffic source without manual reposting.

4. Team Collaboration

Built-in team workspaces let you collaborate on content with role-based permissions. Approvals workflows prevent posts from going live without review — essential for agencies managing client accounts or brands with strict voice guidelines.

5. Built-In Link-in-Bio

Postiz includes a customizable link-in-bio page — the same job Linktree charges $3-15/month for. Point followers from any platform to your Postiz-hosted bio page and track click-through rates alongside your regular post analytics.

6. Analytics and Reporting

Postiz aggregates performance metrics across all connected accounts into unified dashboards. Track engagement rates, follower growth, and per-post performance without opening platform-native analytics. Scheduled reports keep you informed without manual data gathering.

7. API, Webhooks, and MCP Server

For developers and power users, Postiz includes a full public API, webhook support, and an MCP server for AI agent integration. You can automate post creation from external systems, trigger posts based on external events, or build custom reporting pipelines. The CLI tool makes programmatic management straightforward.

8. Self-Hosting via Docker

The open-source Docker deployment means Postiz runs on any server. Official images are available for AMD64 architectures, and community guides cover deployments on Raspberry Pi, cloud VPS instances, and even railway-style platforms. Once deployed, you own the data — no vendor lock-in.

Postiz vs. The Competition

Postiz vs. Buffer

Buffer's strength is simplicity and polish — its interface is widely considered the most intuitive in the category. But Buffer lacks AI content generation, has limited analytics on lower plans, and charges per-user fees that scale fast. Postiz's AI agents and self-hosting option make it a stronger value proposition for technical users or teams willing to invest setup time for long-term savings.

Postiz vs. Metricool

Metricool excels at analytics and reporting — its dashboard depth exceeds what most competitors offer. But it's a closed SaaS with no self-hosting option and its AI features are limited compared to Postiz's agentic approach. Metricool's free plan restricts platforms; Postiz (self-hosted) has no platform caps beyond what the social networks themselves impose.

Postiz vs. Hootsuite

Hootsuite is enterprise-grade but priced accordingly — plans start at $99/month. For small teams and individual creators, it's cost-prohibitive. Postiz delivers scheduling, team collaboration, and basic analytics at a fraction of the cost, with the openness of self-hosting as a bonus for teams with technical capacity.

Postiz Pricing: Cloud vs. Self-Hosted

Postiz offers two paths:

  • Postiz Cloud: $29/month (Standard), $39/month (Team), $49/month (Pro), $99/month (Ultimate). All plans include AI agents, API access, MCP server, and a 7-day free trial.
  • Self-Hosted (Open Source): Free to download and deploy. Your costs are server hosting only — typically $5-20/month for a VPS capable of handling moderate traffic.

The self-hosted option is where Postiz truly differentiates from commercial competitors. For creators managing multiple personal brands or small agencies, the economics are compelling: one $10/month server vs. $150+/month in SaaS subscriptions.

Who Should Use Postiz in 2026

Best fit for: Technical creators, indie hackers, small agencies, and marketers who want full control over their social media infrastructure. If you're comfortable with Docker and a basic server, self-hosting Postiz is the most cost-effective path.
