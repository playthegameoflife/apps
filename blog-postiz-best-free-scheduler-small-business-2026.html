What Is a Social Media Scheduler?

A social media scheduler is a tool that lets you plan, write, schedule, and publish content across multiple social networks from a single dashboard. Instead of logging into Instagram, then Facebook, then LinkedIn separately, you write once and publish everywhere — on a timer you set.

For small businesses, a scheduler means less time doing repetitive posting and more time on strategy, customer service, and actually running the business.

How to Get Postiz Running for Free (Self-Hosted in 10 Minutes)

Self-hosting sounds intimidating, but Postiz's Docker setup takes most people less than 10 minutes. Here's the outline:

Option 1: Deploy on a Budget VPS (~$5/month)

Any VPS with Docker support works. Popular options include Hetzner, DigitalOcean, and Hostinger. Once your server is running, deploy Postiz with:

Docker Compose — one-command setup docker run -d \ --name postiz \ -p 3000:3000 \ -e DATABASE_URL="file:./db" \ -v postiz_data:/app/data \ gitroomhq/postiz-app:latest

After the container starts, open your-server-ip:3000 in a browser and follow the setup wizard. Point your domain's DNS to the server and enable SSL with Nginx Proxy Manager or Caddy for a proper production setup.

Option 2: One-Click Deploy

Postiz supports one-click deployment on Railway, Render, and Coolify. These platforms offer free tiers suitable for small business use. Railway's free tier includes enough resources to run Postiz for a single-business workload.

Connect Your First Platform

Once Postiz is running, go to Settings → Integrations and click "Connect" next to each platform you want to use. Postiz will guide you through the OAuth connection process. For each network, you'll need an existing business or creator account — Postiz uses the platform's official API to post on your behalf.
