Postiz vs Buffer for Solopreneurs in 2026: The Complete Comparison

Published October 15, 2026 · 9 min read

If you run a one-person business and manage multiple social media accounts, you have felt the pain: Buffer charges per channel, Hootsuite charges per user, and the bills add up fast. Postiz — the open-source social media scheduler with 27,000+ GitHub stars — promises to fix that. But is it actually ready for solopreneurs? This guide compares Postiz vs Buffer across pricing, platform support, ease of use, and real-world workflow so you can decide which one belongs in your stack.

What Is Postiz? What Is Buffer?

Postiz is an open-source, self-hostable social media scheduling platform. You run it on your own server (or use their managed cloud), and it publishes to 30+ networks from a single calendar. It includes AI-powered caption generation, a queue system, team collaboration, and analytics. Postiz is actively developed on GitHub with new platform integrations added by the community.

Buffer is a well-established, cloud-only social media management platform. It supports 6 major platforms, offers a clean and intuitive interface, and is known for its simplicity. Buffer has been around since 2010 and is trusted by over 75,000 brands. It charges per channel per month, which is where costs spiral for solopreneurs managing multiple accounts.

This is where the gap is widest. Postiz and Buffer support fundamentally different numbers of platforms:

  • X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest
  • Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads
  • Discord, Telegram, Google Business
  • GitLab, and community-added platforms

Buffer — 6 Platforms

  • Facebook, Instagram
  • X/Twitter, LinkedIn
  • Pinterest, YouTube

If you post to TikTok, Reddit, or any platform beyond the Buffer six, you will need a separate tool or manually cross-post. Postiz eliminates that fragmentation entirely. For solopreneurs who need to be everywhere their audience is, Postiz wins this round by a wide margin.

Pricing: The Real Cost for Solopreneurs

Pricing is where Buffer loses many solopreneurs. Here is the breakdown:

Postiz vs Buffer Pricing at a Glance

  • Postiz (self-hosted): Free. You pay only for your server (~$6/month VPS) and API keys for the platforms you connect.
  • Postiz (cloud): Plans from $29/month after a 7-day free trial. No per-channel fees.
  • Buffer (Essential): Starts at $6/month per channel. Managing 5 channels = $30/month minimum.
  • Buffer (Team): $18/channel/month — per-channel fees scale painfully as you add platforms.

For a solopreneur running 8 social accounts (common if you manage personal + business profiles on multiple platforms), Buffer could cost $48/month or more. Postiz on a self-hosted VPS costs roughly $6/month in hosting — an 8x cost difference. The savings compound annually: $576/year with Buffer vs ~$72/year with a self-hosted Postiz setup.

AI Features: Caption Writing and Content Ideas

Both platforms offer AI-assisted features, but they approach them differently.

Postiz includes a built-in AI agent that can generate post captions, suggest content angles, and help with hashtag optimization — all within the scheduling workflow. You can connect it to your preferred AI provider and generate variations for each platform's optimal format.

Buffer offers AI writing tools through its Add-on plan (AI Creator Suite starting at $6/month extra). The features are solid, but they are paywalled behind an additional subscription layer.

Postiz integrates AI into the core workflow without an additional monthly fee — another advantage for cost-conscious solopreneurs who want AI assistance without accumulating subscription charges.

Ease of Use: Buffer Wins on Simplicity

It would be dishonest to pretend Buffer does not have advantages. Buffer is simpler. There is nothing to install. You create an account, connect your channels, and start scheduling within 10 minutes. The interface is clean, well-designed, and consistently praised for its UX.

Postiz self-hosting requires some technical comfort: you need a server, Docker basics, and API keys for each platform you want to connect. The interface is functional and improving rapidly, but it does not yet match Buffer's polish in every area. If you are non-technical and want zero hassle, Buffer's cloud-only simplicity is a genuine benefit.

The good news: Postiz Cloud offers the same Postiz experience without the server management. You get the full feature set — including self-hosting — on managed infrastructure, starting at $29/month. That is still cheaper than Buffer once you factor in per-channel pricing across multiple platforms.

Self-Hosting: Who Is It For?

Self-hosting Postiz is a meaningful choice only if you value:

  • Data ownership: Your scheduling data, analytics, and content calendar never touch a third-party server.
  • Unlimited accounts: No per-channel pricing. Connect as many profiles as you need.
  • Custom integrations: You control the server, the updates, and the platform integrations you enable.
  • Cost control: Fixed server cost instead of per-channel subscription costs that grow with your presence.

For solopreneurs who are comfortable with a $6–20/month VPS and spending 20–30 minutes on initial setup, self-hosted Postiz is one of the best cost-per-feature deals in the social media tool space today.

Analytics and Reporting

Buffer provides polished, native analytics dashboards with engagement metrics, follower growth charts, and exportable reports. These are useful for presenting to clients or tracking quarterly performance.

Postiz includes analytics within its dashboard, covering post-level performance, queue health, and channel-level metrics. Some users connect Postiz to external BI tools or Google Data Studio for more advanced reporting — a reasonable workaround given the cost savings on the tool itself.

Buffer has the edge here on report polish. Postiz is functional but less visually refined for high-stakes reporting situations.

Team Collaboration

Buffer's Team plan ($18/channel/month) includes multi-user access, approval workflows, and collaborative commenting — designed for agencies managing client accounts.

Postiz includes team features even on its free self-hosted plan: you can invite team members, assign roles, and use approval workflows without paying extra. For small teams and solopreneurs who collaborate with freelancers or virtual assistants, Postiz's approach to teams is significantly more accessible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Postiz really free for solopreneurs?

Yes. Postiz is fully open-source and can be self-hosted on your own server at no cost. You only pay for your server and API keys — there are no Postiz license fees. Cloud plans start at $29/month for those who prefer managed hosting.

How many social platforms does Postiz support compared to Buffer?

Postiz supports 30+ platforms including TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest, Bluesky, Mastodon, Discord, and more. Buffer supports 6 platforms: Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Pinterest, and YouTube.

Can I use Postiz to schedule TikTok posts?

Yes. Postiz supports TikTok scheduling natively. You can upload videos, write captions, set schedule times, and publish directly — all from the Postiz dashboard. Buffer does not support TikTok scheduling.

What is the main advantage of Buffer over Postiz?

Buffer's main advantage is simplicity and managed infrastructure. There is nothing to install, configure, or maintain — you sign up and start posting within minutes. Buffer also has a longer track record and polished mobile apps, making it accessible for non-technical users who do not want to manage servers.

Is Postiz suitable for a one-person business?

Postiz is excellent for solopreneurs who want full control, unlimited accounts, and zero per-channel fees. Running it on a $6/month VPS covers the hosting cost, and you get AI caption generation, team collaboration features, and analytics — without the per-channel pricing that makes Buffer expensive as you scale.
