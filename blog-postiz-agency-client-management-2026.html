Integrations and Automation for Agency Workflows

Modern agencies don't just schedule posts — they build systems. Postiz gives you multiple ways to automate:

REST API

Postiz exposes a full REST API for creating posts, managing queues, fetching analytics, and controlling channels programmatically. If your agency has developers, you can build custom dashboards, sync with your project management tool, or automate post approvals based on client sign-off flows.

Webhooks

Webhooks let external systems trigger actions inside Postiz — or vice versa. When a post goes live, trigger a Slack message to your client's channel. When content is approved in your CMS, automatically queue it in Postiz. Real-time automation without polling.

n8n and Zapier Integration

Postiz integrates with workflow automation tools like n8n (self-hosted or cloud) and Zapier. Build approval workflows where a client's Google Form response becomes a scheduled post. Connect Postiz to your CRM so new client onboarding automatically creates a new workspace and connects their channels.

MCP Server

Postiz also ships with an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, meaning AI agents can interact with your Postiz instance directly. You can build autonomous workflows where an AI agent monitors content performance and automatically queues follow-up posts based on engagement data.

The minimum cost path is a $5–10/month VPS (DigitalOcean, Hetzner, or a cheap dedicated server). Install Docker, run one command, and you have Postiz running on your own infrastructure. The official Docker installation takes under 10 minutes.
