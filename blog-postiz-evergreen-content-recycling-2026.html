What Is Evergreen Content Recycling?

Evergreen content is posts that stay relevant regardless of the date — how-to guides, tips, resource lists, behind-the-scenes looks, and educational material. Unlike newsjacking or trend posts, evergreen posts don't expire.

Content recycling means scheduling those posts to re-publish automatically on a rotating interval — weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly — so your best content gets seen by new followers over time. Instead of manually hunting down old posts and rescheduling them, a smart queue does it for you.

Postiz ReQueue: How It Works

Postiz's ReQueue feature works as a separate publishing queue that draws from a pool of posts you designate as evergreen. Here's the step-by-step:

Step 1: Create and Tag Your Evergreen Posts

When composing a post in Postiz, use the ReQueue toggle to mark it as an evergreen post. Postiz stores this in your content library with a "recurring" label. You can do this retroactively too — go back through your published posts and tag your best performers.

Step 2: Set Your ReQueue Interval

In your Postiz dashboard, configure how often you want each post to recycle. Options typically range from every 7 days to every 90 days. You can set different intervals for different platforms — for example, recycle to X every two weeks, but to LinkedIn monthly.

Step 3: Configure Per-Platform Rules

Postiz lets you define platform-specific behavior for each ReQueue entry:

  • Copy adaptation: Automatically adjust the caption length and hashtags for each network
  • Media handling: Use the same image or swap in a platform-optimized variant
  • Queue priority: Decide whether ReQueue posts should fill gaps or compete with scheduled new content

Step 4: Let It Run and Monitor

Once configured, your ReQueue runs automatically. Postiz pulls from your evergreen pool based on your intervals, generates drafts for review, and publishes them. You spend five minutes approving instead of five hours rescheduling.

Building Your Evergreen Content Library: Where to Start

You don't need 50 posts ready on day one. Start with these five categories that are proven to perform well as evergreen recycled content:

1. How-To Guides and Tutorials

Step-by-step content that answers a recurring question in your niche. "How to schedule TikTok posts," "How to connect your domain to a new platform" — these get searched every day by new users. They never really go stale.

2. Tool Roundup and Resource Lists

"5 tools I use every day for content creation" or "The complete guide to social media scheduling in 2026" — resource lists drive consistent saves, which signal to algorithms that your content is high-value.

3. Behind-the-Scenes and Process Posts

How you built something, your morning workflow, how you plan your week — these humanize your brand and perform well regardless of when they're posted.

4. Frequently Asked Questions

Compile your top 10 DMs or comment questions into a FAQ carousel or post. These speak directly to what your audience is actively asking right now, and that question will still be asked six months from now.

5. Success Stories and Case Studies

Results you've achieved for clients or yourself — follower growth milestones, revenue benchmarks, productivity improvements. Numbers-based posts get saves and shares on a recurring basis.

How Often Should You Recycle Each Post?

There is no universal answer — it depends on your posting frequency and how many evergreen posts you have. Here's a practical framework:

  • Daily posting schedule: Recycle each evergreen post every 30–45 days (gives 30+ posts in rotation)
  • 3–5 posts per week: Recycle every 14–21 days (gives 10–15 posts in rotation)
  • 1–2 posts per week: Recycle every 7–10 days (gives 4–8 posts in rotation)

Watch your analytics. If a recycled post starts getting fewer impressions the second or third time around, it may be too short an interval for that piece. Conversely, if an old post gets stronger engagement on its second life, your audience may be growing fast enough to see it fresh.

What to Do With Non-Evergreen (Trend) Content

Not everything should go into ReQueue. Keep trend-reactive content, news commentary, and event-based posts in your regular calendar only. Postiz lets you maintain two separate queues:

  • ReQueue (evergreen): Your rotating autopilot pool
  • Scheduled: One-time posts queued for specific dates

This two-queue system is the secret to creators who post consistently without burning out. The evergreen pool holds the fort; your scheduled posts capture trend moments.
