What Are Postiz and Buffer?

Postiz is an open-source, self-hostable social media scheduling and automation platform. It supports 30+ platforms, includes AI-assisted caption generation, and runs entirely on your own server via Docker. You can use it completely free by self-hosting, or opt for Postiz's managed cloud plan at $15/month flat — no per-user fees.

Buffer is a cloud-based social media management platform founded in 2010. It offers scheduling, analytics, and engagement tools across a curated set of platforms. Buffer operates on a per-user subscription model, with plans starting at $6 per user per month (Essentials) and scaling up to $12 per user per month (Growth) for advanced features.

Notable platform quirks to know

Regardless of which tool you choose, some platforms impose API-level restrictions that affect scheduling:

  • TikTok: You cannot pick a sound via any third-party scheduler's API — this is a TikTok API limitation, not a tool-specific issue. Photo posts on TikTok also reject PNG files in some cases.
  • Instagram: Postiz supports both feed posts and Reels via the official Instagram Graph API. Buffer also supports these with some limitations on story scheduling in lower tiers.
  • LinkedIn: Both tools support Company Pages, but personal profile posting requires additional OAuth permissions on both platforms.

Self-Hosting: Postiz's Killer Feature

The ability to run Postiz on your own server is its defining advantage. When you self-host Postiz:

  • Your data is yours. No third-party SaaS has access to your posting history, analytics, or audience data.
  • No per-seat licensing. Add as many team members as you want without triggering a billing event.
  • Full API access. Postiz exposes a REST API on every plan — including self-hosted — so you can build custom integrations, connect to n8n or Zapier workflows, or pull data into your own dashboards.
  • No vendor lock-in. If Postiz's roadmap changes direction, you have the source code. You can fork it, customize it, or self-maintain it indefinitely.

Buffer has no self-hosted option whatsoever. Everything lives on Buffer's infrastructure. For many businesses this is fine — but for agencies handling sensitive client data or regulated industries, it's a compliance consideration worth weighing.

AI Features: Built-In vs Add-On

Both tools offer AI-assisted caption writing, but the implementation differs significantly.

Postiz includes AI caption generation in its core interface. You can generate post variations, adjust tone, and rewrite copy without leaving the app. The feature works on both self-hosted and managed plans.

Buffer offers an AI assistant called Buffer AI, but it's gated behind the Growth plan ($12/user/month). On the Essentials plan, you write captions manually. Buffer AI can help generate post ideas and optimize posting times based on your audience's engagement patterns.

For creators who rely heavily on AI for content throughput, Postiz delivers this capability at a lower price point. The value difference is especially stark when you're paying $12/seat for Buffer AI features you could access on Postiz's $15/month flat plan.

Analytics and Reporting

FAQ: Postiz vs Buffer

Is Postiz really free to use?

Postiz is open source and free to self-host on your own server. The core features cost nothing — you only pay for hosting (a $5–10 VPS). Postiz also offers a managed cloud plan starting at $15/month for those who prefer not to manage their own infrastructure.

How many social media accounts can I connect on Postiz vs Buffer?

Postiz has no per-account limits — connect as many accounts as you need across all supported platforms. Buffer's Essentials plan starts at $6 per user per month but caps you at 8 social accounts, and the $12 per user per month plan allows 25 accounts. For creators managing multiple brands, Postiz is effectively unlimited.

Does Buffer have a self-hosted option?

No. Buffer is a fully cloud-based SaaS product with no self-hosted version. Your data lives on Buffer's servers. If data ownership and privacy are priorities, Postiz's self-hosted model gives you full control — your database, your server, your rules.

Which tool supports more platforms: Postiz or Buffer?

Postiz supports 30+ platforms including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, Bluesky, Mastodon, LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest, Google Business, Discord, GitLab, and more. Buffer supports the major platforms but at a smaller count, with some specialized platforms not available.

Can I use AI to generate social media captions in Postiz?

Yes. Postiz includes built-in AI-assisted caption generation — write, improve, and adapt post copy directly within the scheduling interface. Buffer also offers an AI assistant, but it requires a higher-tier subscription. Postiz's AI features are available on both self-hosted and managed plans.

What's the best choice for a small team on a budget?

For small teams or solo creators on a tight budget, Postiz is the stronger choice — run it on a $5–10 VPS and pay no per-user fees. Buffer's per-user pricing scales expensively at $6 per seat. If you need enterprise features like client approval workflows and dedicated support and don't want to manage your own server, Buffer's higher tiers deliver those out of the box.
