AI Features: Caption Writing, Idea Generation, Media Creation

Both Buffer and Postiz have leaned into AI since 2024, but their approaches differ fundamentally.

Buffer's AI is a managed service. You open their AI assistant, type a prompt, and get a caption. It's convenient and requires zero setup — but you're locked into their model choice, their pricing, and their content guidelines. The quality is solid for casual users.

Self-Hosting: Why It Matters and How Easy It Actually Is

The phrase "self-hosting" can sound intimidating if you've never touched a server. But Postiz's Docker Compose setup has been refined significantly since its early releases — and it's genuinely approachable for non-engineers.

Here's what you need to know:

  • What you get: A complete, private instance of Postiz running on a $5–15/month VPS. No data leaves your server. No Postiz branding required.
  • What you need: A basic VPS (1GB RAM works for small setups, 2GB+ recommended), a domain name, and about 30 minutes of setup time following the official Docker Compose guide.
  • Updates: Pull the latest Docker image, restart the container. No manual patching.
  • Backup: Your PostgreSQL database and uploaded media live on your server. You own them.

If that still sounds like too much engineering for your team, Postiz also offers a hosted cloud version at postiz.pro — same software, zero server management. The trade-off: monthly subscription returns, but it's still often cheaper than Buffer at scale.

Where Buffer Wins

Honesty matters in a comparison. Buffer has genuine strengths Postiz can't fully match today:

  • Setup speed: Create an account and connect your first channel in 3 minutes. No server, no domain, no Docker. For absolute beginners, Buffer wins on friction.
  • Mobile app: Buffer's iOS and Android apps are polished. Postiz is primarily web-based; mobile scheduling requires the browser.
  • Browser extension: The Buffer browser extension lets you share any webpage with one click. Postiz has no equivalent.
  • Ecosystem integrations: Buffer plays nicely with Canva, Giphy, and other creative tools out of the box.
  • Known brand: Explaining Postiz to a client still requires a 30-second "it's like Buffer but open source" explanation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Postiz really free to use?

The self-hosted version of Postiz is 100% free under the AGPL-3.0 open source license. You only pay for your server costs — roughly $5–15/month for a VPS. Buffer starts at $6/channel/month, which adds up fast for multiple accounts.

Can I self-host Postiz instead of paying monthly?

Yes. Postiz runs on Docker Compose on any VPS (DigitalOcean, Hetzner, AWS). One $15/month server handles unlimited channels, team members, and posts. Buffer's agency plan can run $100+/month for the same capability.

Does Postiz support as many platforms as Buffer?

Postiz supports 28+ platforms including X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Bluesky, Reddit, Pinterest, Discord, Mastodon, and more. Buffer covers the major platforms but has fewer niche network options.

Which tool has better AI features — Postiz or Buffer?

Both offer AI caption generation, but Postiz gives you full control over which AI model to use (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, or any LLM via API). Buffer's AI is locked to their managed service. Postiz also lets you self-host the entire stack for maximum data privacy.

Can I migrate from Buffer to Postiz?

There's no automatic one-click import tool, but you can manually re-create your scheduled posts in Postiz. Because Postiz uses official OAuth connections to each platform, your existing social accounts simply reconnect — no data loss on the platform side.

Is Postiz good for agencies managing multiple clients?

Postiz is particularly strong for agencies. Unlimited users, unlimited channels, and a flat server cost mean client management scales linearly with your server bill — not with each new brand you add. Buffer's per-channel model punishes growth.
