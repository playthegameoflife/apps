Task: summarize the report for the team.
Instructions:
1. Start with a two-sentence overview.
2. List the three most important findings, each with one sentence on why it matters.
3. Note any risks or open questions.
Format: plain language, no jargon, under 200 words.
If something is unclear in the report, say so instead of guessing.
