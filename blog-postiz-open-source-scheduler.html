What Is an Open Source Social Media Scheduler?

An open source social media scheduler is a scheduling tool whose source code is publicly available, allowing you to host it on your own server. Unlike SaaS tools that lock your data on their servers, self-hosted schedulers let you own everything — your content calendars, analytics, and audience data.

The appeal is straightforward: no subscription fees, no per-user pricing, and full control over where your data lives. For creators and teams tired of watching SaaS pricing climb every year, open source schedulers have become the smart alternative.

Why SaaS Schedulers Are Bleeding You Dry

Let's be direct about what you're actually paying for with tools like Hootsuite and Buffer:

  • Hootsuite: $99/user/month minimum for teams. For a 5-person team that's $5,940/year — before add-ons, API overages, or extra social accounts.
  • Buffer: Starts at $6/user/month but caps features at higher tiers. The "Advanced" plan needed for full analytics runs $120/user/month.
  • Later: Built primarily for Instagram; limited platform support at lower tiers.

All of them charge per seat, enforce API rate limits, and store your data on their infrastructure. When they raise prices — and they will — you either pay or migrate everything under pressure.
