What Is Postiz?

Postiz is an open-source social media scheduling platform licensed under AGPL-3.0. It was built as a self-hostable alternative to commercial tools like Buffer, Later, and Hootsuite — giving creators and teams full ownership of their scheduling infrastructure without recurring subscription fees.

The platform connects to 30+ social networks including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, Pinterest, Google Business, GitLab, Mastodon, Bluesky, Threads, and Discord. A built-in AI agent drafts post captions, generates images, and creates short videos — all from a single dashboard with a visual calendar.

Self-Hosting via Docker

Postiz runs on a single Docker container. The official repository at github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app provides a docker-compose file that spins up the full stack — database, backend, and frontend — in under five minutes. You control the server, the data, and the upgrades.

MCP Server and CLI

For developers, Postiz exposes a public API, an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, and a CLI. This means you can drive Postiz programmatically from your own AI agents, connect it to n8n or Make.com workflows, or build custom publishing pipelines. This level of extensibility doesn't exist in Buffer or Hootsuite outside of expensive enterprise plans.

Team Collaboration

Postiz includes role-based access control, approval workflows, and a shared media library. The Team plan ($39/month managed, or self-hosted free) supports unlimited team members, post approval queues, and granular calendar permissions — making it practical for agencies managing multiple client accounts.
