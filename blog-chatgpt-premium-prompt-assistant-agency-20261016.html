4 Common Prompting Mistakes Agency Teams Make

  • Missing Role Definition: ChatGPT doesn't know who it's supposed to be. Without explicit role framing ("You are an experienced marketing strategist..."), the AI defaults to generic consultant mode and produces generic outputs.
  • Vague Output Specifications: "Write something good" produces something mediocre. Professional prompts specify format, length, tone, and structure — then let the AI execute with precision.
  • No Context Layering: The best ChatGPT outputs come from progressive context addition. Professional prompters build context incrementally rather than dumping everything in one message.
  • Ignoring Iteration: The best outputs come from treating AI interaction as a conversation, not a single-shot request. Premium prompting includes refinement prompts for complex deliverables.
