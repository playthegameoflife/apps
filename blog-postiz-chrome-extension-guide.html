What Is the Postiz Chrome Extension?

The Postiz Chrome extension is a lightweight browser add-on that lets you create, preview, and schedule social media posts without opening the full Postiz dashboard. It lives in your toolbar, works on any tab, and connects to your Postiz account — whether you're running the self-hosted open-source version or Postiz Cloud.

You browse, you see something worth posting about, you click the extension icon, and within seconds your content is queued for the right platforms at the right time. No context switching. No logging into another tab. No losing the link you wanted to share.

How to Install the Postiz Chrome Extension

Step 1: Find It in the Chrome Web Store

The easiest path is the Postiz listing on the Chrome Web Store. Search "Postiz" or follow that direct link and click Add to Chrome.

Step 2: Open Postiz in a Tab

Before the extension works, keep a Postiz tab open in your browser. The extension communicates with your Postiz instance through this tab — think of it as a local bridge between the extension and your scheduler.

Step 3: Authenticate via OAuth

Click the Postiz icon in your browser toolbar. The extension will prompt you to sign in with OAuth — the same login you use for Postiz. Once authenticated, the extension is ready to use across all tabs.

Self-Hosted Setup Note

If you're running your own Postiz Docker instance, add the following environment variable before starting the container, then rebuild:

How to Use the Postiz Chrome Extension: 30-Second Workflow

1. Capture the Content

Browse to any page, article, video, or image you want to share. Click the Postiz icon in your toolbar. The extension opens and automatically captures the page URL and title — no copy-paste required.

2. Write or Adjust Your Caption

The extension pre-fills the page title as a starting point. Edit the caption directly in the extension popover. You can also paste raw text, a thread you've drafted, or a caption you've written elsewhere.

3. Attach Media

Drag and drop images or videos directly into the extension popover, or click to browse your files. The extension supports standard image formats (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF) and video formats for supported platforms.

4. Select Channels

Check the boxes for every platform you want to post to. You can post to one channel or all of them at once — Postiz handles platform-specific formatting and character limits automatically.

5. Pick a Time

Choose Now for instant publishing or pick a future date and time. Postiz respects each platform's best posting windows, and you can set default queue times in your Postiz dashboard.

6. Queue It

Click Schedule. Postiz adds your post to the queue. You'll see it appear in your Postiz calendar under the correct date and time.

What the Extension Cannot Do (Yet)

Be clear-eyed about the extension's scope. It handles the drafting and scheduling workflow, but Postiz's richer features live in the dashboard:

  • Analytics — Engagement metrics, reach, and impressions require the full Postiz dashboard.
  • AI post generation — The AI agent that auto-generates platform-specific captions and images runs inside the dashboard, not the extension.
  • Team collaboration — Approval workflows, team assignments, and role-based permissions are dashboard features.
  • Webhook and API integrations — Connecting Postiz to n8n, Zapier, or custom workflows is configured in the dashboard settings.

Think of the extension as the capture layer for your social media workflow. Once a post is in the queue, the full Postiz platform takes over for everything downstream.
