Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Overview

Prompt Helper ("we", "us", "our") provides an AI prompt enhancer and generator available as a web app at promptgenerator.fyi and as a Chrome extension. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information we collect

Account information (optional): If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google user ID, email address, and display name from Google's userinfo endpoint. This is used to identify your account and sync your subscription.

Prompt data: When you use the generator, your prompt text and the selected mode/style are sent to our backend (Cloud Run) for enhancement. We do not store your prompts on our servers — they are processed in real time and returned to you.

Usage data: We track how many enhancements you've used this week (stored locally in your browser). We do not send usage analytics to our servers.

Payment information: If you upgrade to Pro, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your credit card details. We store your Stripe customer ID and subscription status in our database (Firestore).

Local storage: We store your user ID, usage counter, history, and preferences in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device.

How we use your information

  • To provide and improve the prompt enhancement service.
  • To identify your account and sync your subscription across devices.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions via Stripe.
  • To enforce free-tier usage limits.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe: For payment processing. Subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API: For prompt enhancement. Prompts are sent to Google's Gemini API for processing.
  • GitHub Pages: For hosting. Subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Data retention

Account and subscription data is retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your data by contacting us. Local storage data can be cleared by clearing your browser data.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of data sale (we don't sell data).
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. The only cookies are those set by third-party services (Stripe, Google) during authentication and payment flows.

Children's privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or data requests, contact us at: support@promptgenerator.fyi
