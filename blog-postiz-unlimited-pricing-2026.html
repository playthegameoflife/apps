The Social Media Pricing Problem Nobody Talks About

Most social media schedulers built their pricing model when creators had two accounts: a Facebook page and a Twitter handle. That model made sense in 2013. Smaller brand, younger product

Postiz has over 33,000 GitHub stars and an active community, but Buffer and Hootsuite have been around since 2010. If you need enterprise-level compliance, dedicated support SLAs, or integrations with legacy enterprise tools, those platforms have deeper ecosystem coverage.

Self-hosting means self-support

If you run Postiz on your own Docker instance, you're responsible for updates, backups, and uptime. The Postiz community is helpful, but there's no 24/7 enterprise support ticket. For the Cloud version, this is handled for you.
