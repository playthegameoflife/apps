Questions, answered

Which AI chats does Prompt Helper Gemini work with?

ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, Grok, and Perplexity. Once you're signed in, the Improve button appears right next to the send button in each chat — no copy-pasting between tabs.

What do I get for free?

5 prompt enhancements and 5 Ask questions per week, plus limited prompt history. No credit card required — install it and sign in with Google.

What does Pro add?

Unlimited enhancements, unlimited Ask questions, and your full prompt history — tied to your account, so it works across your devices. You can upgrade anytime from the Pro tab inside the extension.

Do I need an account?

Yes — a free Google sign-in, so your plan and history follow you. Setup takes about 30 seconds.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome and Chromium-based browsers — Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera, and Vivaldi — via the Chrome Web Store.

What happens to my prompts?

Enhancements are processed over a secure connection to provide the service, and your settings and preferences are stored locally in your browser where applicable. The details are in our privacy policy.
