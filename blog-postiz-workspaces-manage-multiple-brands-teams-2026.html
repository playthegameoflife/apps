What Are Postiz Workspaces?

A workspace in Postiz is a self-contained environment for one brand, client, or business unit. Each workspace holds its own connected social accounts, content calendar, team members, queues, and analytics — completely isolated from every other workspace on the same Postiz instance. Think of it as a virtual operating company for each of your brands, all running inside one installation.

Whether you run one personal brand, manage content for three local businesses, or operate a social media agency with dozens of clients, workspaces let you scale without multiplying your tool stack. You log in once, switch between workspaces in seconds, and never accidentally post to the wrong brand account.

Setting Up Your First Postiz Workspace

Getting a workspace running takes under five minutes if you already have Postiz deployed. The workflow is designed to be self-service, with clear steps from account creation to your first scheduled post.

Step 1: Create the Workspace

In your Postiz dashboard, navigate to Settings and select "Workspaces." Click "New Workspace," give it a descriptive name (e.g., "Acme Fitness Brand" or "Client: Bloom Cosmetics"), and optionally upload a logo so your team can visually distinguish it at a glance.

Step 2: Connect Social Accounts

Within the workspace, go to Connected Accounts and authorize each platform you need. Postiz supports OAuth connections for all major platforms — no need to hand over personal login credentials. Supported networks per workspace include:

  • YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Threads
  • X (formerly Twitter), LinkedIn (personal and Company Pages), and Reddit
  • Pinterest, Google Business Profile, and GitLab

Step 3: Invite Team Members with the Right Roles

This is where Postiz workspaces genuinely pull ahead of simpler schedulers. Postiz supports role-based access control at the workspace level:

  • Admin — full control: manage team, billing, and all content
  • Editor — create, schedule, and publish posts; cannot manage team or billing
  • Viewer — read-only access to analytics and calendar; useful for clients who want visibility without editing rights

Invite team members by email, assign their role per workspace, and they see only the brands you gave them access to. This makes freelancer onboarding fast and auditable.

The Content Calendar: Your Workspace Command Center

Each Postiz workspace includes a visual content calendar showing every scheduled post across all connected accounts. You see at a glance which days are packed, which are empty, and where your posting frequency is inconsistent — critical for maintaining the regular cadences that social algorithms reward.

Posts live in queues before they go live. You set a queue order, and Postiz publishes according to your schedule — perfect for building a consistent posting rhythm without manual intervention every day.

How to Structure Your Agency Workflow with Postiz Workspaces

For agency owners, the workspace model aligns naturally with a client onboarding process. Here's a practical setup that works for teams handling 5–30 active clients:

Per-Client Workspace

Create one workspace per client from day one, even if they're currently on a single-platform plan. This means onboarding a new platform for that client is a workspace setting change, not a migration. It also means client data never leaks between workspaces.

Internal Agency Workspace

Keep one workspace for your agency's own social accounts. Running agency and client content in separate workspaces means your team's posting calendar never competes with client deliverables, and your agency's analytics stay clean.

Review and Approval Queues

Use Postiz's team review feature inside each workspace. Content goes into a pending state, a senior editor or client approver reviews it, and only then does it move to the live queue. This workflow eliminates the back-and-forth of email-based approvals and keeps all content history auditable.
